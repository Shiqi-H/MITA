Orto Botanico Città Studi

Via Camillo Golgi 18, 20133 Milano, Italy

The Città Studi Botanical Garden was created in 2001 on the reclaimed land of the former Cascina Rosa farmstead in Milan. Spanning roughly 25,000 square metres, it brings together plant collections by growth environment, human use, and survival strategy, including carnivorous plants, succulents, and reconstructed habitats typical of the Lombardy region.
